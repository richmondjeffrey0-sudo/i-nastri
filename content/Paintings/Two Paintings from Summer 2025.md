I painted both of these spur-of-the-moment, over two afternoons (both extremely hot days) in my studio in Pawtucket, RI. I only had that studio for a few months, but I fondly remember locking myself in a room with nothing but junk / random objects / flowers / bottles and a big mirror for several hours a day on a regular basis. 
These two paintings came out of nowhere, to me, but they're some of my favorites from that summer. They were painted against a desiccating southern sunlight (perhaps the least ideal lighting condition for painting), but I think that leaves them with a distinctive imprint of the season they were painted in.
